**벨만 최적 방정식**

$$
\begin{aligned}
v_*(s) &= \max_a \sum_{s'} p(s' \mid s, a) \{ r(s, a, s') + \gamma v_*(s') \} \\
q_*(s, a) &= \sum_{s'} p(s' \mid s, a) \{ r(s, a, s') + \gamma \max_{a'} q_*(s', a') \}
\end{aligned}
$$

**최적 정책**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mu_*(s) &= \operatorname{argmax}_a q_*(s, a) \\
&= \operatorname{argmax}_a \sum_{s'} p(s' \mid s, a) \{ r(s, a, s') + \gamma v_*(s') \}
\end{aligned}
$$

System diagram illustrating 'Generalized Policy Iteration (GPI) with Action-Value Function Q(s, a)'.
A dynamic circular cycle with two major alternating phases:
1. Left Half (Policy Evaluation): '몬테카를로 정책 평가 (Policy Evaluation)'
   - Simulates episodes using current policy π
   - Updates Q(s, a) using sample mean of returns G
2. Right Half (Policy Improvement): '입실론-탐욕 정책 개선 (Policy Improvement)'
   - Greedily improves policy: a* = argmax Q(s, a)
   - Guarantees ongoing exploration with probability ε (10%)
Center core: 'Optimal Policy π* and Optimal Value Q* Convergence Anchor'.

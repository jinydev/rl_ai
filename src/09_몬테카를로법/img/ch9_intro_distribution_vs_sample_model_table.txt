Comparative overview card: 'Distribution Model vs Sample Model in Reinforcement Learning'.
Two symmetrical comparison panels side-by-side:
- Left Panel (Distribution Model / DP):
  - Requires full transition probability p and reward r
  - Analytical summation: E[X] = ∑ x · p(x)
  - Limited to small, toy simulation problems
- Right Panel (Sample Model / MC):
  - Zero model requirement; only requires interaction simulator
  - Arithmetic sample mean: E[X] ≈ (1/N) ∑ x_i
  - Scales to real-world complex problems, games, robotics
Bottom summary banner: 'From theoretical distribution math to practical sample-based empirical learning'.

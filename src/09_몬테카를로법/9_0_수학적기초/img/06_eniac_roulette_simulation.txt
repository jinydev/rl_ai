Studio Ghibli aesthetic 2D animation style, masterwork educational conceptual illustration. 1940s vintage computing laboratory filled with towering racks of warm glowing vacuum tubes, patch cables, and analog dials of an early electronic computer. In the center, a luminous conceptual fusion: a spinning golden casino roulette wheel and cards emitting sparkling trails of binary numbers (0 and 1) and random sampling data streams into the computing circuits. In the bottom corner, Jiny the tuxedo cat wearing tiny glasses and Toto the fluffy white puppy with a sky-blue bandana watch the historical computational birth of Monte Carlo simulation with wonder. Warm ambient vintage tones, detailed linework, aspect ratio 16:9 wide.
Negative: Japanese text, modern laptops, 3D CGI, dark horror.

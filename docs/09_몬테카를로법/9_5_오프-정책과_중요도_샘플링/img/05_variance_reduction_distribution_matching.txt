Scene:
Visualizing variance reduction in Importance Sampling:
When behavior distribution b is distant from target π, the importance ratio ρ = 2.4 blows up a sample of 3 to 7.2, causing massive instability.
When behavior distribution b is gently brought closer to target π ([0.2, 0.2, 0.6] approaching [0.1, 0.1, 0.8]), importance ratio ρ stabilizes near 1.0, and variance dramatically plummets from 10.63 down to 2.48.

Studio Ghibli style, warm hand-drawn 2D animation aesthetic, Hayao Miyazaki inspired, soft watercolor and cel shading, gentle lighting, whimsical atmosphere.

Pure white background, clean isolated backdrop, absolute solid white #FFFFFF, no background scenery, no gradients.

Characters:
1. Dorothy (7-year-old Korean girl, wearing yellow hoodie and red hair ribbon, weighing timeline hourglasses on a brass balance scale).
2. Jiny (magical blue sprite fairy hovering near the scale, adding glowing golden dust to the most recent hourglass).

Scene:
A conceptual comparison between Sample Average (Equal Weights 1/n) and Exponential Moving Average with fixed learning rate α.
On the left: Past and current samples all have the same flat, fading weight (1/n).
On the right: Recent samples receive high exponential weight, rapidly fading out ancient outdated samples.
Chalk text on a wooden easel reads: 'Non-Stationary Adaptation: Q ← Q + α(G - Q)'.

Composition:
1:1 square canvas, centered balance scale and weight graphs, soft pastel tones, warm Ghibli feeling, clean isolated white background, no Japanese text.
